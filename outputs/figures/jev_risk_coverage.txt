Note. Based on 983 first-pass valid outputs and inverse-probability design weights. The blue line shows expected error at each coverage threshold when retaining the most confident observations; exactly tied confidences are fractionally averaged, independently of row order. The grey ribbon shows pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 2,000 stratified Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates. The coral dashed line denotes expected error under random selection, with its 95% interval as a faint coral band. The excluded low-confidence observations are not assumed to have been successfully human-reviewed. Coverage and error are conditional on the 983 valid first-pass responses, not on all 1,000 attempts, and exclude the 17 invalid outputs and all diagnostic reruns. No disagreements were observed through 60% automated coverage on the plotted grid. In this region, the bootstrap interval can collapse to zero because resampling cannot produce errors absent from the retained observations; zero observed risk must not be interpreted as a zero true error probability.
