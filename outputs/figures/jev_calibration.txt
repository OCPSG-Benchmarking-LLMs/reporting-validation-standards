Note. JEV top-class calibration relative to the automated OCPSG silver reference. All 983 valid first-pass outputs are inverse-probability weighted. Observed accuracy and mean confidence are summarised in prespecified 0.1-wide probability bins; error bars are pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 2,000 stratified Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates. The orange dashed line marks perfect calibration; the lower panel shows each bin's weighted share among valid outcomes. Unweighted observations by confidence bin: 0.3–0.4: n=2, 0.4–0.5: n=16, 0.5–0.6: n=42, 0.6–0.7: n=42, 0.7–0.8: n=44, 0.8–0.9: n=67, 0.9–1.0: n=770. Nonempty bootstrap replicate counts are provided in calibration_figure_data.csv. The low-confidence bins have few cases, so their estimates and bootstrap intervals are imprecise. The 17 invalid first-pass responses and diagnostic reruns are excluded; this does not evaluate calibration against independent human labels.
