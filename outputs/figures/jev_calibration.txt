JEV Top-Class Calibration against the Automated OCPSG Silver Reference

Note. All 983 valid first-pass outputs are inverse-probability weighted. Observed accuracy and mean confidence are summarised in prespecified 0.1-wide probability bins; error bars are pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 2,000 stratified Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates. The coral dashed line marks perfect calibration, and the lower panel shows each bin's weighted share among valid outcomes. Unweighted observations by confidence bin: 0.3–0.4: 2; 0.4–0.5: 16; 0.5–0.6: 42; 0.6–0.7: 42; 0.7–0.8: 44; 0.8–0.9: 67; 0.9–1.0: 770. The low-confidence bins have few cases, so their estimates and intervals are imprecise. The 17 invalid first-pass responses and diagnostic reruns are excluded; the figure does not evaluate calibration against independent human labels.
