Validation Performance across Prompt Specifications and Repeated Inference

Note. Each panel reports a design-weighted operational validation metric against the OCPSG silver reference: Accuracy, Macro-Precision, Macro-Recall and Macro-F1. Within each model group, the four frozen prompt conditions occupy separate lines and are distinguished by colour. Each circle is the prompt-level estimate, in which the three repetitions of a speech are averaged so that the speech carries its sampling weight once, and the line through it is the pointwise 95% percentile interval from 2,000 stratified Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates (the same replicates as the primary P0 analysis). The thicker bar on each line spans the lowest to the highest of the three single-repetition estimates. Models are shown in the fixed experimental order rather than sorted by performance, and the x-axis keeps the original 0–1 metric scale.
