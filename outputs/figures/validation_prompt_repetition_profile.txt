Validation Performance across Prompt Specifications and Repeated Inference

Note. Each panel reports a design-weighted operational validation metric against the OCPSG silver reference: Accuracy, Macro-Precision, Macro-Recall and Macro-F1. Within each model group, the four frozen prompt conditions occupy separate lines and are distinguished by colour. Each circle is the prompt-level estimate, in which the three repetitions of a speech are averaged so that the speech carries its sampling weight once, and the line through it is the pointwise 95% percentile interval from 2,000 stratified Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates (the same replicates as the primary P0 analysis). The three single-repetition estimates of a prompt differ by a median of 0.006 and at most 0.059 (macro-precision for Mistral Large 3 under P3). Models are ordered by their primary P0 macro-F1 estimate; because many intervals overlap, the order is not a ranking. The x-axis keeps the original 0–1 metric scale.
