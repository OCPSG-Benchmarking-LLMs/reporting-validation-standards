Counterfactual Electricity-Related Emissions for Qwen3.6 (35B) and Gemma 4 (31B) across National Electricity Grids

Note. Measured machine-level CodeCarbon energy for 12,000 classifications in the Netherlands (Qwen3.6 (35B): 1.724909 kWh; Gemma 4 (31B): 3.231545 kWh) is held constant across countries and multiplied by the annual national-average lifecycle electricity carbon intensity reported in CodeCarbon v3.3.1 (country data years vary). Both panels share one colour scale in g CO2e per 1,000 classifications, a functional unit rather than a directly observed per-request footprint. In total, 168 countries with matched Natural Earth 1:110m boundaries are coloured; hatched grey areas have no matching country factor, and some microstates are not drawn at this resolution. Country differences are counterfactual location assumptions, not measurements in each country. The analysis excludes hosted API models and does not estimate energy or emissions from their token usage or latency. Hardware embodied emissions, transport, location-dependent cooling, grid time-of-day variation and differences in hardware are excluded. Source: measured production summary, CodeCarbon country factors v3.3.1 and Natural Earth 1:110m public-domain boundaries.
